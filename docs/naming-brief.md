# Naming brief

**Product.** Fund data crossing a system boundary while keeping its evidence: outputs trace to source rows, accounting meaning survives the mapping, gaps carry a named decision, the destination proves it accepted the load.

**User.** A fund accountant at the incoming administrator. Non-technical, lives in Excel, distrusts black boxes. Call 1: "I cannot trust any number I get from them"; "does this number foot to that number".

**Register.** Trustworthy, precise, quietly dry. Sponsor Ylookup is a VLOOKUP pun: a wink at accounting culture is welcome, a joke is not.

**Constraints.** One or two words, single word at most 9 letters. Same in English and German. No digits. No bare generic finance noun. Nothing used by eFront, Canoe, Accelex, Allvue, FIS, Investran, Yardi, Ylookup, or known software.
